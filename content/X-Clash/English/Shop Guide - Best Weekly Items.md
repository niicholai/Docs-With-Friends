### What is this for?

Use this guide to manage game currencies effectively. It lists priority items for the VIP, Union, Honor, and Trial shops. These shops refresh periodically. Buy the recommended items to maximize value. Save remaining currency for the next refresh cycle.


---

## VIP Shop

> [!INFO] Refresh Schedule This shop refreshes weekly.

Purchase these items in order of priority:

1. VIP 30 Days: Buy this only if you do not own the Monthly VIP Pack. (Cost: 10,000 Diamonds)
2. Stamina
3. Advanced Relocators
4. Speedups
5. Gold Hero Exp Chests

> [!TIP] Resource Management | Buy these items every week. Save remaining Diamonds for the next week. You also need Diamonds for Kingdom events.


---

## Union Store

> [!INFO] Refresh Schedule This shop refreshes weekly.

Purchase these items in order of priority:

1. S+ Universal Shards
2. Mystic Beast Breakthrough Potions
3. Advanced Relocators and Union Relocators
4. Building Speedups (8h and 1h)
5. Technology Speedups (8h and 1h)
6. Training Speedups (1h)
7. Healing Speedups (1h)

> [!TIP] Purchase Strategy | Buy urgent items first. Buy the remaining recommended items before the reset. Save your remaining Union Contribution points for the next week. Do not buy unlisted items.


---

## Honor Shop

> [!INFO] Refresh Schedule This shop refreshes monthly.

Purchase these items in order of priority:

1. S+ Universal Shards
2. Orange Decoration Chest
3. Universal Decoration Stone
4. Mystic Beast Breakthrough Potion

> [!CAUTION] Early Game Strategy | Do not buy Legendary Gear Scrolls in the early game. Save War Merits for future needs.


---

## Trial Shop 

> [!INFO] Refresh Schedule This shop refreshes weekly.

Purchase these items in order of priority:

1. S+ Universal Shards
2. Lvl 3 Mystic Beast Mark Chest
3. Legendary Trial Chest (Always buy this item)
4. Mystic Beast Breakthrough Potion
5. Upgrade Crystal
6. Universal Decoration Stone

> [!TIP] Excess Currency | Use extra Trial Coins to buy Mystic Beast EXP Potions. Save the remaining coins to maintain a steady supply.


---

## Strategy Summary

1. S+ Shards: Always prioritize these items.
2. Speedups: Buy all available speedups.
3. Relocators: Purchase relocators for active wars.
4. Mystic Beast Potions: Hoard these items for the late game.


---

## Budget Management

Follow the numerical order of the lists. Item number 1 has the highest priority. Skip lower priority items if you lack sufficient currency. Calculate costs to ensure efficiency.


---

## Big Spenders & Late Game

> [!INFO] Target Audience | This section applies to high-spending players. It applies to the top 20 players in the server Peak Arena. It also applies to the top 50 players in the server group Peak Arena.


### Honor Shop Priority

`S+ Universal Shards` Purchase S+ Universal Shards.

> [!NOTE] Diminishing Returns | The value of these shards decreases as you maximize your S+ Heroes.

`Legendary Gear Scrolls` Purchase Legendary Gear Scrolls. High-spending players reach the late game faster than other players. You require the following quantities:
- 50 scrolls per gear piece.
- 200 scrolls per Hero.
- 1,000 scrolls per team.


### Restricted Items

`Decorations:`
> [!WARNING] Do Not Purchase | Do not purchase decorations or decoration stones from the Honor Shop. Do not spend 50,000 War Merits on a random decoration. Only purchase decorations if you can select the specific item.

`Breakthrough Potions` Purchase Mystic Beast Breakthrough Potions only for immediate requirements. Example: Buy small quantities to complete a level breakthrough.
