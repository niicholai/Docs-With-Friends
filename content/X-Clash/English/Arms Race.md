## Arms Race Event



### Unlock Conditions

> [!INFO] Requirements | You must reach Castle Level 7. The server must be active for at least 3 days.


### Event Structure

The event cycle repeats daily. It consists of 6 rounds. Each round lasts for 4 hours. Each round features a different theme.


### Rewards System

Complete daily goals to fill the progress bar. Each round contains 3 treasure chests. Earn personal Arms Badges to unlock these chests. Chests contain rewards and additional Arms Badges.


### Daily Grand Chest

Collect Arms Badges to unlock the Daily Arms Race Grand Chest. This chest has three tiers. The tiers unlock at 2, 8, and 18 total Badges.

> [!TIP] Efficiency Strategy | You can earn a maximum of 6 Arms Badges per round. Complete 3 rounds to unlock all daily chests.
