### What is VS?

The Versus Duel (VS) is a weekly event. It begins after the server reaches a specific age. The system matches the Union against an opposing Union. Opponents may come from the same server or different servers. Matchmaking is randomized. Expect varying difficulty levels each week.


### Event Schedule

The event lasts for 6 days. It includes one rest day. The event day starts at 00:00 Server Time (UTC-2). Players earn points for themselves and the Union. Each day focuses on specific tasks. Examples include Mystic Beast training or Technology Research.

## Free Use: Non-Scoring Items

> [!WARNING] No Points Awarded | Do not save the following items for VS Events. These items do not generate points.

- Trial Chest
    
- Badge Chest
    
- Decoration Chest
    
- Lucky Supply Crate
    
- Gear Chest
    
- Gear Crystals
    
- Upgrade Crystals

Use these items at any time.

## Day 1: Intelligence Training 

1. `Intelligence Quest:` Complete quests before the event starts.

> [!CAUTION] Collection Timing | Do not collect the rewards yet. Wait for the VS event to start before opening completed quests.

2. `Resource Gathering:` Dispatch teams to gather Food, Iron, and Gold. Start this hours before the Server Reset.

3. `Arms Race (Mystic Beast):` Use Stamina, Mystic Beast Breakthroughs, and EXP Potions. Coordinate this with the "Arms Race: Mystic Beast Training" schedule.

4. `Arms Race (Hero):` Use Hero EXP. Coordinate this with the "Arms Race: Enhance Hero" schedule.


---

## Day 2: Castle Construction 

1. `Intelligence Quest:` Do **not** perform Intelligence Quests on this day.

2. `General Tasks:` Perform the following tasks at any time:
	- Dispatch Legendary Wagons.
	- Complete Gold Tavern Quests.
	- Perform Survivor Recruitment.

3. `Building Upgrades:` Upgrade buildings during "Arms Race: City Construction."

> [!TIP] Maximizing Points | You can start upgrades at any time. Do not tap the unwrapped present when they finish. Open completed upgrades only during the Arms Race. Save Intelligence Quests for Day 3.


---

## Day 3: Technology Research 

1. `Mystic Beast Mark Chest:` Open these chests on this day.

2. `Energy Crystals:` Use Energy Crystals during "Arms Race: Technology Research."

> [!INFO] Exclusive Use | Save Energy Crystals exclusively for Day 3.


---

## Day 4: Hero Upgrade 

1. `General Tasks:` Use Hero Shards and Skill Exp Books at any time.

2. `Arms Race:` Perform Hero Recruitment and Hero Exp usage. Coordinate this with **"Arms Race: Enhance Hero.**"

3. `Preparation:` Save Intelligence Quests for Day 5.


---

## Day 5: Full Preparation 

1. `Combined Actions:` Perform all actions from Days 1 through 4. Align these actions with their respective Arms Races.

2. `Soldier Training:` Train soldiers. Use saved resources and speedups for this day.


---

## Day 6: Defeat the Enemy 

1. `Hospital:` Heal wounded troops when Day 6 begins. Do not heal them the day before.

2. `Combat:` Kill enemy soldiers to earn points. Higher-level soldiers grant more points. Losing your own troops also grants points.


---

## Day 7: Rest Day

1. `Resource Management:` Save all resources.

2. `Preparation:` Save Intelligence Quests for Day 1 of the next cycle.
