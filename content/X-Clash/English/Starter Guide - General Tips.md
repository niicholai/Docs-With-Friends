## Take Your Time

> [!INFO] Speed Running | Do not speed run the game. Rushing leads to mistakes. Rushing wastes resources.

Play at a steady pace. Reaching higher castle levels takes months. Prioritize knowledge over speed. Learn the correct priorities for research, buildings, and heroes. This strategy saves resources in the future. Proper prioritization is valuable.


---

## Daily Tasks

Complete daily tasks every day. These tasks include:
- Tavern Quests
- Caravan Wagons
- Arms Race Chests
- VS Tasks
- World Boss
- Faction Trial
- Peak Arena
- 3V3 Arena
- Event Tasks (During Events)

Completion takes 30 to 60 minutes. You can complete them throughout the day. The rewards provide essential resources.


---

## Resource Collection

Collect resources upon every login. Perform the following actions:
- Tap resource icons to collect.
- Collect AFK resources.
- Collect resources from the inbox.

> [!TIP] AFK Limits | Collect AFK rewards before they reach the cap. This prevents production loss.


---

## Turn On Notifications 

Enable specific push notifications. Go to **Profile Picture > Settings Tab > Push Notifications**. Activate critical notifications.

Recommended Notifications:
- Building Upgrade
- Technology Resource
- Resource Output
- AFK Rewards
- Under Attack
- Scouted
- Arms Race Start
- Shield Expiration

These alerts prevent soldier loss. They prevent giving points to the enemy. They indicate when to start new tasks.


---

## Do Not Overextend

Do not exceed necessary point limits. Exceeding limits is wasteful for most players.

`VS Event Limits` VS chests stop at 7.2 million points. Ensure you have unlocked the upper tiers via research.

`Arms Race Limits` Arms Race rewards stop at 60,000 points (Chest 3). Collect 18 Arms Badges to unlock the final daily chest.

> [!WARNING] Efficiency | Only exceed limits to secure first place. Otherwise, stop at the final reward chest.


---

## Stamina Management

Manage stamina carefully. Do not attack Zombies or Elite Zombies excessively. Do not attack other players excessively. This wastes time and stamina.

Priority Stamina Uses:
- Intelligence Quests
- Elite Zombies
- Zombie Overlords
- Events
- VS War

Intelligence Quests consume significant stamina. VS War requires a minimum of 100 stamina. Save stamina for these priority activities.


---

## Do Not Punch Above Your Weight Class

> [!CAUTION] Overlord Rallies | Do not attack Zombie Overlords beyond your strength. Only start or join rallies you can win.

Check the recommended CP before the fight. Losing a fight destroys soldiers. The Overlord may counter-attack your castle. This wastes time and resources. Failed rallies also waste Union member resources. Fight only targets you can defeat.


---

## Union Boss and World Boss

The World Boss and Union Boss differ from standard enemies. These bosses possess unlimited HP. You cannot defeat them. The objective is to inflict damage within a specific time limit. Higher damage values unlock higher reward tiers.

> [!INFO] Union Boss Participation Requirements | Participate regardless of your power level. Failure to participate results in lost rewards. Your attacks contribute to the Union's total damage.


---

## Inventory Management (Stock Up)

Do not open inventory items immediately. Stockpile chests, Diamonds, and VS Crystals. Open these items only when necessary.

`Resource Scaling` Resource chests scale with Castle Level. Higher levels yield more resources from the same chest.

`Usage Method` Select the "Upgrade" button on a building. Open only the required resources when prompted by the system. This maximizes resource gain over time.
